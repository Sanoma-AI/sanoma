Choose where your company’s workflows and configuration will live. You’ll need Node 26 or later.

- **New repository.** [Start a company in an empty directory](/start/start-a-company/). The installer creates a Git repository with an Outbound collection, a sample workflow, and a scenario to test it.
- **Existing repository.** [Install Sanoma in your repository](/start/install/). You choose the connectors and add the configuration alongside your code.

Once the local app opens, [run your first workflow](/start/first-workflow/) to see how a scenario, a workflow, and its ledger fit together.
