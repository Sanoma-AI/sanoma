---
title: "Sign in to your company"
description: "Open a hosted company using your staff account or an invitation link."
sidebar:
  order: 1
---

Open the app address your company gave you. If you received an invite link, open that link to sign in.

Otherwise, use a sign-in method shown on the company’s sign-in page.

- **Email code.** Enter the email address your company has on file for you. Open the message, enter the code on the sign-in page, and submit it.
- **GitHub.** Choose GitHub and sign in with the account your company recognizes.

If you can’t get in, ask the person who manages **People** to check your entry and send an invite. An administrator can also [check which staff sign-in methods the host offers](/using-the-app/go-live/#configure-staff-sign-in).

From **Home**, choose **Workflows** to [find and run a workflow](/using-the-app/runs/) or **Inbox** to [review an approval](/using-the-app/approvals/).
