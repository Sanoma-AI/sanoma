# packages/sanoma/src

The source of `sanoma`: the bin (`cli.ts`, `dist/cli.js` once built) and the modules it is made of, and the package's root entry, `index.ts`; its other entries point at its parts' modules (see the [package's guide](../AGENTS.md)).
