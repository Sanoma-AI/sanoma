# packages/workflows/src

The source of `@sanoma-ai/workflows`: how connectors, workflows and policies are declared, how a worker runs them durably on DBOS, and the tools that read them (lint, outline, describe). Its entry points, from `exports` in `package.json`: `.` (`index.ts`), `./data`, `./describe`, `./fake`, `./plugin`, `./resources` (`secret.ts`), `./scenario`, `./scenarios` and `./shared`.
