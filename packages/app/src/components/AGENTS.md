## Layout conventions

- Spacing scale: `gap-(--gap-region)` between a page's regions, `gap-(--card-spacing)` inside a card (Card's own variable), `gap-(--gap-section)` inside a section (a titled one is a `Region`), `gap-2` inside an item.
- Compact (`data-density="compact"` on `#app`, chosen under Density in the user menu) re-sets, in `src/style.css`, Tailwind's spacing unit, `--spacing`, on the page beside the sidebar (`[data-slot="sidebar-inset"]`), so every padding, margin, gap and height utility there tightens with it (the sidebar, with its fixed widths, and menus, dialogs and tooltips outside `#app` keep the full unit); and it re-sets the first three tokens: the two gap tokens on `#app` (worked out in the full unit) and Card's `--card-spacing` in two unlayered rules. The `compact:` variant is for shape (the graph box's height and header padding, the rail's one-line rows, the strip's squares, `Facts`' rows), not spacing.
- Every pane of a workflow's page is a [`WorkflowView`](workflow-view.tsx): its notices, the graph card beside the 20rem inspector (one column while the pane itself is narrower than `@3xl`), then the tabs.
