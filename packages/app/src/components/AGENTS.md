## Layout conventions

- Spacing scale: `gap-(--gap-region)` between a page's regions, `gap-(--card-spacing)` inside a card (Card's own variable), `gap-(--gap-section)` inside a section (a titled one is a `Region`), `gap-2` inside an item.
- Compact (`data-density="compact"` on `#app`, chosen under Density in the user menu) re-sets Tailwind's spacing unit, `--spacing`, on `#app` in `src/style.css`, so every padding, margin, gap and height utility inside the app tightens with it (menus, dialogs and tooltips render outside `#app` and keep it); it also re-sets the first three tokens, the two gap tokens on `#app` and Card's `--card-spacing` in two unlayered rules. The `compact:` variant is for shape (the graph box's height and header padding, the rail's one-line rows, the strip's squares, `Facts`' rows), not spacing.
- Every pane of a workflow's page is a [`Workbench`](workbench.tsx): its notices, the graph card, then the primary and secondary columns (3 : 2), one column while the pane itself is narrower than `@3xl`.
